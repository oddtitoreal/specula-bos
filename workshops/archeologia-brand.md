# Brand Archaeology

**Layer**: 03 · IDENTITY — Brand Core  
**Duration**: 3 hours + 1 hour AI analysis, plus corpus preparation

---

## Objective

Separate radical values from contingent values through a narrative stress test. The resulting DNA is the output of a test that can fail.

## How It Works

**Preparation, the corpus of traces**: decisions reconstructed from documents (minutes, budgets, offers declined) as well as from the stories of those who made them; individual strategic interviews with different roles, external stakeholders included; an inventory of official futures, what the organization has publicly said about its own future; counterexamples, decisions that betrayed a declared value; a measure of how much the organization has changed over the last ten years.

**Phase 1**: the group organizes the corpus: what the brand has said, what it has done, where the two diverge. AI analyzes the corpus to identify recurring archetypes and inconsistencies between declared values and behaviors.

**Phase 2, Value Stress Test**: each candidate value is first written in operational form ("if it does not improve X, it is not a valid choice"), then projected into the four scenarios. A value holds if, in every scenario, it still distinguishes between options when doing so is costly. People who did not build the scenarios judge whether it holds: an external reviewer or a second team. Causal Layered Analysis works as a second method, reading each value at the level of worldview and metaphor. At least one candidate value must fall: if every value holds, the test has stressed nothing.

**Phase 3**: the Synthesis Circle deliberates on the ethical tensions that emerged and defines the red lines.

## Expected Output

Speculative Brand DNA with classified values (radical, fragile, contingent), at least three documented decisions for each radical value, a counterexample log, and explicit red lines.
