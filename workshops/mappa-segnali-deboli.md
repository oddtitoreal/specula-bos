# Weak Signals Map

**Layer**: 01 · WORLD — Signal Scan  
**Duration**: 3 hours

---

## Objective

Collectively build a map of weak signals relevant to the brand, classified by domain and urgency.

## How It Works

Each participant brings five weak signals observed in their domain of expertise; at least one third of the signals come from domains outside the brand's sector. The collection grid is declared: the five domains (cultural, technological, regulatory, economic, environmental) arranged around the research question set in the Brief Under Stress. The group works in two rounds: in the first, signals are placed in their domains and assessed for distance from the question; in the second, implication chains are built, tracing how each signal could influence others and what it would mean for the brand if it consolidated. AI is used to identify non-obvious correlations between signals from different domains. Collection stays human, because models tend to surface signals that are already frequent.

## Expected Output

Shared Weak Signals Map with 15–20 classified signals, each tagged with source and domain, and their first- and second-level implications.
