# From Prototype to Protocol

**Layer**: 05 · STRATEGY — Strategic Protocol  
**Duration**: 4 hours

---

## Objective

Design the preferable future and distill from the validated prototypes the decision criteria that will constitute the brand's strategic protocol.

## How It Works

The Synthesis Circle works on five levels:

1. **Preferable future**: from prototypes, values and scenarios, the Circle designs the future the brand will work toward. The scenarios describe what could happen; the preferable future states the chosen direction, as a project of its own.
2. **Backcasting**: for each scenario, the group works back from the preferable future to today and identifies the steps that hold in that world.
3. **Narrative thesis** of the brand, built from the constellation of prototypes, values and preferable future.
4. **Criteria as hypotheses**: the thesis becomes operational choice criteria, the rules of thumb for evaluating future decisions. Values remain the stable reference; each criterion carries an expected effect and a review date and is recorded in Decision Memory.
5. **Experiment roadmap** at 30/60/90 days to test hypotheses in real contexts before they become irreversible choices. Experiments are designed so that they can fail without damage.

The restitution to leadership is designed as a design moment: a synthetic, tangible presentation of the choices, built to be decided on.

## Expected Output

Preferable future with a backcasting map; Strategic Protocol with narrative thesis, decision criteria (each with expected effect and review date), and experiment roadmap.
