# Collaborative Worldbuilding

**Layer**: 02 · FUTURES — Scenario Engine  
**Duration**: 4 hours

---

## Objective

Build four divergent scenarios from the drivers identified in the Signal Scan, making them concrete through everyday life vignettes. The scenarios are the test bed from which Brand Archaeology derives the DNA, so their divergence is guaranteed by a construction rule.

## Construction Rule

- **Four scenarios, one per generic future**: growth, collapse, discipline, transformation (Dator, *Alternative Futures at the Manoa School*, Journal of Futures Studies 14(2), 2009).
- **Closed megatrends appear in every scenario.** Scenarios diverge only on the open variables.
- **One scenario is built against the brand's strongest declared value**: a world where that value becomes costly, is co-opted, or loses relevance.
- **At least one scenario should look implausible to the client at first reading.** A scenario that looks reasonable to everyone usually extends the present.
- **Horizon of 10–15 years** (for example 2036–2040). Values need a long window to be tested; the decisions drawn from the scenarios are later translated into 1–3 year actions.

## How It Works

The facilitator presents the driver matrix and separates closed megatrends from open variables. The group splits into four teams of at least two people, each assigned to one generic future; for this workshop, external participants join the Circle. Each team writes a *day in the life* of a typical user in its scenario, identifies three plausible newspaper headlines from that world, and defines materialization indicators. The four teams present to each other, then together identify white spaces: futures no competitor is credibly occupying, each labelled as trend or countertrend.

## Expected Output

Four complete scenarios with vignettes, headlines, materialization indicators, declared closed megatrends and open variables, and a white spaces map.
